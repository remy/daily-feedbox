# Feedbox brief — Friday 20 November 2026

Thursday's brief asked who gets to read your messages. Signal has named a new reader: the assistant on your phone. It is [blocking screenshots][signal] on iOS 27 and Android, because Siri and Gemini scan the screen. You can switch the block off, but Signal says that **breaks its own guarantee**. It cannot block them on macOS at all, where [Gemini gained computer use][verge-gemini] on Thursday. Whittaker calls this **"an existential threat to privacy at the application layer"** ([The Verge][verge-signal]).

Mozilla says [not one non-WebKit browser has shipped on iOS][moz], nearly three years after Apple's EU conditions and almost a year after Japan's. **Shipping Gecko would mean a second Firefox app**, with users migrating bookmarks and passwords by hand. Matthew Somerville found the domestic version. The "Open as Web App" toggle in iOS 26 **overrides his PWA manifest**, then never sets the CSS `display-mode` to match ([dracos.co.uk][dracos]). He cannot detect the state he is in.

Monday's brief watched for Obsidian-to-Bear scripts after Bear Blog shipped its API, and two arrived. [openpluto][pluto] built a QuickAdd command that imports posts with their Bear UID. It **checks the remote timestamp before overwriting newer work**. Scheduled and live posts raise separate warnings, and new posts are always drafts. [wmprkr][wmprkr] is close to the same thing from the command palette. **Neither wants to open a browser to publish.**

Last.fm will require a Pro subscription for API keys from 1 December. One reader [closed a 2005 account][codito] with **123,000 scrobbles** and moved to ListenBrainz. Listening, a local-first music server written up twice on Friday, [leans on ListenBrainz too][listening] for related artists. It also [keys each recording by file hash][listening-room], so one copy serves every playlist. Elsewhere a dead Mac mini has [put Plex in question][disquiet]. **The files are the easy part. The metadata is what you rent.**

## Talking points

1. **A Riemann proof that nobody has checked.** Anthropic's Levent Alpöge says he proved the Riemann hypothesis with Claude, formalised in Lean. The Clay Institute requires two years of scrutiny and general acceptance first. Anthropic itself said in August that it did not expect those techniques to get there. He held publication until after Thursday's IPO. [The Verge][verge-riemann]

2. **Agents close bugs without emptying the backlog.** An Ask HN thread works out why. Agents patch the symptom in the report, not the class of bug behind it. Pinning down the real fix in English costs roughly what writing it would. [Hacker News][askhn]

3. **`flex-wrap: balance` has shipped in Chrome 150.** It fixes the item that wraps alone, with no media query to break when the content changes. It is Chrome-only for now, so the write-up carries the old workaround as well. [ishadeed.com][ishadeed]

4. **Repairability still depends on being believed.** A 3,000 EUR Framework 16 arrived broken. The owner used Framework's own tool to show the keyboard reporting a numpad ID. Framework replaced the midplate instead, replaced the keyboard two months later, and closed the case "No Fault Found". [poolp.org][poolp]
