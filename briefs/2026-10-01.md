# Feedbox brief — Thursday 1 October 2026

Reddit will switch off RSS on 13 November, and blames scraping. Moderators are pointed at Discord apps. For everyone else there is **no replacement at all**. The developer behind [Underpass App][lapcat] intends to quit that day, and his support subreddit goes with him. Others just rebuilt what they needed. [Nolan Lawson][nolan] left WordPress.com after fifteen years for a static site. [Mike Hall][mikehall] **faked two WordPress endpoints** to prise a webhook out of Libsyn.

Wednesday's brief covered six firms signing a voluntary AI pledge with no penalties. Harder scrutiny has arrived too. The [FTC][ftc] is now probing OpenAI, Anthropic and others over product dangers. The Bank of England's governor [told the BBC][bbc] that markets have **priced everybody as a winner**. He reached for Netscape to make the point. Google held [Gemini 4 Argon][gemini] back to **trusted cyber defenders**.

Wednesday's thread on writing gained its hardest evidence. [Jon Seager][jnsgr] had a model draft his last few work newsletters, guided by his own back catalogue. A colleague spotted it in a 360 appraisal, and said they carried **less of his voice**. Seager now reads the generated draft, throws it away, and writes. [Satisfice][satisfice] sees the same drift in engineering: most of the time goes on documents, not code.

Several posts went the other way, and kept old things running. [Tyler Sticka][sticka] writes on a MacBook Air bought in 2011, now on Linux Mint. [56k.rip][fiftysix] simulates 1996 dial-up against a real **byte budget**, so a slower line really does take longer. Nearly every sound is built in the browser from a written score. On [leadedsolder][forth], a Sega SG-1000 owner is **writing his first Forth**, made to sit copyright-free in ROM.

## Talking points

1. **Firefox 157's new look has an off switch.** The Nova engine broke [Dan Q's vertical tabs][danq], and [creolened][creole] hit the same wall. Both landed on one fix: set `browser.nova.enabled` to false.
2. **Mathematicians have set terms for AI-generated proofs.** Over 600 replies shaped [the advice][agmai]. A lab that publishes a result nobody understands must fund the understanding later, and must not steer it.
3. **Calling AI boring is the hopeful reading.** [Christopher Butler][butler] notes that electricity turned dull through decades of argument over who owned the wires. [MOND←TECH][mond] takes the darker line: cheap intelligence makes workers easy to replace.
4. **Apple's 72-hour inactivity reboot may already be beaten.** A leaked video has Magnet Forensics claiming it can freeze iPhones so GrayKey reads them. [404 Media][graykey] has the claim, not proof.
