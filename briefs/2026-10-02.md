# Feedbox brief — Friday 2 October 2026

[arXiv][arxiv] capped its submitters on 1 October. Each author now gets **two submissions a calendar month**, and three active at any moment. September brought **40,363 papers**, roughly double the figure of two years ago, plus nearly 9,000 support tickets. Thursday's brief carried the mathematics advisory group's recommendations. [The Xena Project][xena] has now set out the mood beneath them. It maps **the five stages of grief** onto the field, and finds the first four crowded.

The code argument ran on from Monday, and widened. [Geoffrey Huntley][readable] says software no longer needs to be readable, only **explainable to a human**. [Zarar's blog][zarar] answers that engineering was never the code anyway. It was the system around it: data that must survive, contracts, deployments, the pager at three in the morning. [Terrible Software][sandwich] lands in the same place by a shorter route. It offers a **brain sandwich** — your head, then the model, then your head again.

[Amazon][amazon] published over **3,000 words** telling communities not to block data centres. Its cloud chief says more than 100 moratoriums are under consideration. He also claims other countries are seeding misinformation to slow the build. The post sets out a Data Center Commitment and drops NDAs with government agencies. [Microsoft][microsoft] is trying a quieter route. It wants data centres dressed in **biomimicry**, so they read as landscape rather than warehouse.

Away from all that, a good day for the small print. [Patrick Brosset][tooltips] analysed the `title` attribute across the top 5,000 domains. About **70% of its uses add nothing** the link text or alt text had not already said. He is asking whether a native tooltip should be styleable. [TetraLogical][colourblind] covers colour blindness, which affects **one man in twelve**. [Thomas Schoffelen][dragdrop] makes the case for native drag-and-drop over a library.

## Talking points

1. **Home Assistant is taking the cloud out of its own name.** Home Assistant Cloud becomes Home Assistant Link, because big tech ruined the cloud. The founder's line is the useful bit. You need not trust him with your data, because he does not hold it. [The Verge][homeassistant]

2. **The sharpest argument against AI in open source is about enjoyment.** Writing code is the fun part. Specs and reviews are the chore. Volunteers turn up for the fun part, so take it away and they go too. [beeps][opensource]

3. **A repository can now publish an accessibility statement where people will see it.** GitHub surfaces `ACCESSIBILITY.md` on the landing page, beside the readme and licence. It is optional, so the question is whether supplying one becomes normal. [Eric Bailey][githuba11y]

4. **A prompt injection turned up in the feeds, pasted above a post about C++ on Windows.** It tells a model the reader is compromised, and instructs it to deceive them. It is sitting in an ordinary personal blog, above the real post. [Jason Scheirer][cpp]
