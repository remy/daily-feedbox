# Feedbox brief — Monday 12 October 2026

Thursday's brief counted the people holding up key projects. [Coding Alone][alone] counts the ones who left. Mark Padgham sampled npm, PyPI, CRAN, JOSS and rOpenSci. Since January 2021, commits fell 18–57% and distinct issue authors fell 36–64%. Comments on issues fell 51–84%. **Community is shrinking faster than code.** First-time arrivals drop hardest, and non-core helpers faster than core. Public repositories now read more like one person's mirror.

[Releasing AI-generated software][ploeh] asks who answers when nobody read the code. Robert C. Martin had said he no longer reads what his agents write; he has since walked that back. The post's claim is not about quality. Human review never caught everything either. It is that **blame still lands on a person.** Denmark approved Tesla FSD in June with the driver still responsible, facing a manslaughter charge if someone dies.

Thursday's brief ran the ACD RFC on what "supported" should mean. [The dataset itself][acd] is now on show. It pairs each browser with each screen reader — JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver — and marks the pair supported, partial, unsupported or untested. The published matrix is illustrative, not real data yet. It ships machine-readable JSON, working with Mozilla, the WebDX group behind BCD, and ARIA-AT. The pitch: **"supported" does not mean "accessible".** An [Open Collective page][oc] is logging £5 contributions.

Two Monday posts shipped something built by hand. [A ZX Spectrum demoscene intro][speccy] begun on 13 December 2025 came to 10,287 bytes of Z80: scrolltext, three-channel AY tune, VU meters, parallax starfield. That is **about 455 bytes a week.** [A commercial web product][cgi] went out as a C binary on OpenBSD, served by CGI, with SQLite, Stripe and no required JavaScript. **One binary, three dependencies.**

## Talking points

1. **A single interior photo now resolves to a street address.** [404 Media][raven] found Raven, sold to the LAPD and Miami-Dade, matching rooms against Zillow, Airbnb and Vrbo listings. Its chief executive says they never trained it to do that.

2. **The auditor says the models cannot be audited.** Cathy O'Neil started an algorithmic auditing firm ten years ago. [Her essay][audit] argues an audit needs a narrow use case and predictable behaviour, and that general models give neither.

3. **Engineers are the mechanics, not the Luddites.** [Sean Goedecke][ludd] answers the post Friday's brief ran: software has always automated other people's trades. The smiths who built the power looms were themselves replaced fifty years later.

4. **Icons can be plain design tokens.** Lea Verou's [case for choosing icons in CSS][icons] now ships as [@nudeui/icons][nudeui]. Set `--icon-start` and a 1em masked pseudo-element follows your colour and font size.
