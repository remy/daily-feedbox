# Feedbox brief — Tuesday 24 November 2026

Who gets to reach your inbox ran through Monday and Tuesday. [Kagi Mail][kagi] opened a public beta on Monday. Its sender screening **holds new senders until you approve them**. [Pixel Envy][pxl] quotes Paul Kafasis on AI cold mail swamping spam filters, and wishes email had come with **digital postage**. Herman keeps contact forms off [Bear][herman] blogs, because the effort of sending an email filters out the impulse. [Karl Koch][koch] pictures agents writing to agents, with nobody reading either side.

Two posts say AI has become the job rather than the tool. [Jason O'Neil][oneil] borrows the strangler fig, which wraps a tree until it replaces it. The wiki turns into agent context. **The mission statement no longer describes the work.** [Scott Smitelli][smit] rejects Linus Torvalds's claim that LLMs are just compilers. He argues a compiler only translates a decision you already made. An agent lets you **dodge the decision and still sign the work**.

The disclosure argument from Sunday's brief moved from proofs to people. [Peter Cameron][cameron] says the AI answers to his open problems have become a flood. He will **list the claims but not check them**. [Jacob Filipp][filipp] admires an AI-built blog but cannot tell which ideas were its author's. [Jeff Kaufman][jefftk] now tags each post with Claude's share. A typical post runs **3% of words and 10% of ideas**.

Two reference sources are going dark. [Project Gutenberg][pg] has stayed offline since the week before. A Bluesky post the blogger quotes blames a **harness-assisted attack with planted back doors**, built up over months. Monday's brief covered agent hacking tools. Separately, Arm's lawyers told the man behind an [AArch64 reference site][arm] to take it down. It closes on **4 December**, and he is looking for ways to keep links working.

## Talking points

1. **New York has banned paying again for hardware you already own.** The [new law][ny] stops car makers charging subscriptions for built-in features like heated seats. It takes effect on 27 February 2027.
2. **An old iPod nano can now boot Rockbox with no computer attached.** [Viktor Krapivin][ipod] chained a font exploit into an untethered boot on the nano 5G. His aim was a current music player that never goes online.
3. **Ladybird ran almost everything Simon Willison threw at it.** He [tried the alpha][simon] that Monday's brief covered. WebAssembly tools worked. Datasette Lite failed on a missing service worker call, and he filed the bug.
4. **"Because I can" is not a reason.** [Karl Koch][koch] asks for the nerve to leave a possible automation undone. He would rather keep doing the part that teaches him what he thinks.
