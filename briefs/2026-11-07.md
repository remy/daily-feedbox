# Feedbox brief — Saturday 7 November 2026

Friday's brief read Anthropic's S-1 for its numbers. The same filing says the company may soon cross a safety line it wrote itself. [METR spent eleven weeks inside Anthropic][verge-metr] to check. It found the models improving about **twice as fast** as a year ago, mostly because of AI rather than extra compute. Staff self-reported being twelve times as productive. Anthropic then [redacted **most of the evidence METR found useful**][po-metr].

Monday's brief ran the argument for reading machine-written code. Saturday's feeds move it to **who signs the work**. [Andrei Draganescu][draganescu] says credit and blame both belong to the author, not the tool. [Daniel Miessler][miessler] draws the line at understanding, not at AI use. [Codemanship][codemanship] says the number of tries shows **your quality bar**, not your skill. [Swizec Teller][swizec] goes the other way, and reads outcomes first and code last.

Agent sandboxes rest on microVMs. [Replit said on Wednesday][replit] that it rebuilt its sandbox engine on them. On Saturday [pwn.ai published a **Firecracker escape**][pwn]: root on the host, from inside a guest. The trick is to overwrite a vCPU thread's saved registers. The thread then returns where the attacker wants. Firecracker kept running, and the VM **stayed reachable over SSH**.

The personal web spent Saturday arguing about readers. [Unattributed.cc][unattributed] replies to Candy Ether at length and says **audience size is no measure of worth**. [Sam Wilson][samwilson] hardened his site against crawlers and ended up leaning more on big tech. [Anarchaeopteryx][arch-cost] listed a year online in Canadian dollars, down to the laptop. [Laura Michet][lauramichet] followed Misty's archiving guide, which Monday's brief ran, and **downloaded her own site**.

## Talking points

1. **Most public agent skills do nothing.** [One benchmark tested 98 of them][skills]. 92 made no real difference, and the average pass ratio moved from 76.6% to 76.9%. Curated skills did lift another benchmark from 33.9% to 50.5%. So drop the ones that only restate your docs.

2. **A connector's edge is its OAuth client, not its URL.** [ChatGPT routed a follow-up call through a second connector without telling the user][connector]. It carried a one-time code issued by the first. The server refused it only because both connectors came from one provider. The reporter then walked away from the bug bounty, which wanted an ID card up front.

3. **Sodium-ion stops being a lab story this year.** [Wood Mackenzie puts cell parity with LFP at late 2026][sodium] — $229/kWh against roughly $200 — with a 21% lower total cost over twenty years. Supply deals already cover over 70 GWh. Module and system parity waits until after 2030.

4. **A pop-up with no way out is a bug, not a quirk.** [Marcin Wichary works through every way out of one in iPhone photo search][wichary]. Swiping does nothing, tapping a photo opens it, and either X drops the search. Three things work in the end. None feels right.
