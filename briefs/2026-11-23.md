# Feedbox brief — Monday 23 November 2026

A factory setting left on stopped every departing flight in America. On The Verge's [Decoder][faa], Darryl Campbell traces it to a router swap in El Dorado, Arkansas. The new fibre router assumed a high-speed network, and flooded a 1960s switch with connection requests. **Seventeen of the 22 en route centres went dark.** Sunday's brief carried the claim that reliability follows from retained engineers. Campbell says the FAA staff who oversaw contractor work were cut in 2025.

Nvidia [put Jensen Huang back in charge][cnbc] on Monday. The vote was unanimous. As [The Verge notes][verge], the board had said on 17 November that he was not coming back. The [board says][nv] it **took input from senior levels of the United States government**. On that basis it ruled out a national security risk. The White House says Trump was ["totally involved"][wh]. The inquiry into the reported spy plot goes on.

Two Monday posts treat AI plumbing as attack surface. [Senko Rašić][senko] reads the canary-token research from Sunday's brief. His practical line: OpenRouter's ZDR flag is **a wish, not a hard rule**. Pin the providers you trust by name. [404 Media][artex] covers the FBI warning about ARTEX, a Go framework that runs hacking agents. Its author withdrew the repository in October. Clones kept publishing it. One host says the code is already copied everywhere.

Monday said two things about who sets the web platform. [Ladybird][lb] reached alpha for macOS and Linux, on **its own engine rather than a fork**. It ships on a rolling channel, supports WebDriver and the Firefox DevTools protocol, and asks site authors to report breakage. [Amit Merchant][amit] wrote up Chrome's new HTML insertion and streaming methods, `appendHTML` among them. They read better than `insertAdjacentHTML`. [Caniuse][ciu] has them in **Chromium 157 and nowhere else**.

## Talking points

1. **Owning up is harder than being hacked.** [Troy Hunt][hibp] had Claude read 8.5 years of his breach mailbox: 73 firms, one reached only after six hops between inboxes. In the worst threads, lawyers argued that telling customers was itself the harm.
2. **An advisory is not an incident report.** [Kyle Reddoch][kyle] works CVE-2026-124194 from the help desk end. Microsoft rates it moderate and CISA lists it as exploited. The question that matters is whether the user opened the file or only used the preview pane.
3. **Take your SSH keys off disk.** [jola.dev][ssh] kept private keys in a password manager and still loaded the copy on disk. Code that scans a disk finds that copy. The keys now live in a Bitwarden agent.
4. **Steve Faulkner has rebuilt HTML5Doctor because the old site turned to slop.** He says [it][h5d] now runs pieces under his name that he did not write. Clean copies of the old archive sit on GitHub while he readies a domain.
