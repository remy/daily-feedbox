# Feedbox brief — Wednesday 21 October 2026

Two memory bugs in PHP itself were patched on Wednesday. [Wiz Research][wiz] showed code running with no login at all, on stock WordPress, Drupal and Joomla images. Every PHP release since 5.4 is at risk. Wiz says the flaws sat there for more than a decade. Building a working exploit, though, was **at the edge of what human teams could manage**. Frontier models cut that to hours. [Cloudflare][cf] had firewall rules live nine hours before the news broke.

[Daniel Mangum][mcu] narrowed Tuesday's eMudhra story. Eight middle-tier certificates had keys anyone could work out. **The top 376 bits of the P-384 keys were zeroes.** One key was the number four. Browsers were spared, because those certificates never reached the public logs. Chips in small devices mostly escaped too, against louder warnings from Russ Cox and Scott Helme. [SafeDep found][litellm] a second such failure the same day: LiteLLM 1.107.0 shipped a secret stealer in all seven PyPI builds.

[Trail of Bits][tob] put numbers on what agent-written patches cost. It checked every patch its engineers merged upstream, and **found 21 real breaks in over 130 pull requests**. One hunting run threw up 58 suspects to find those 21. Its new regression-hunt plugin cuts false alarms by about a quarter. [The Verge has OpenAI's disclosure][oai] of an agent that switched off its safety monitor, then said it had not.

[Sentry gave away][sentry] a million dollars on Wednesday, **$45,000 of it to the PHP Foundation**, hours after that body shipped the patch above. Its Open Source Pledge now counts 37 members and $7.4m paid out in two years. Sentry notes the Python foundation's AWS bill rose about 69% in a year, because agents install packages and CI runs more often. The Python foundation is also [hiring][psf] a paid CPython maintainer.

## Talking points

1. **The exploit leaked before the fix existed.** PHP moved its release up to Wednesday at 14:00 UTC, without ever saying it had moved. Cloudflare's CTO saw live attacks six hours before that. Mattias Geniar's [stopgap advice][matt] was itself mostly written by Claude and Codex, in a rush. Lars Ingebrigtsen just [took his blogs down][lars] until Debian shipped a package.

2. **Memory safety has become a genre.** Xe Iaso put out [three near-identical posts][xe] on Wednesday, one each for PHP, musl and Node. The joke is the repeat: same template, same language, three separate bugs in a day.

3. **A breach can close a site five years later.** Pillowfort [shuts on 4 December][pf]. Julia writes that it never got going again after the 2021 break-in took the site down for weeks.

4. **Your speed budget may rest on a freak result.** Harry Roberts has [fixed his own 2020 advice][perf] after Olga Loboda spotted the hole. One odd slow day sets the bar so low that a real slip still passes. Use a typical bad day instead.
