# Feedbox brief — Monday 26 October 2026

[CSS-Tricks][css-tricks] is **independent again**. Geoff Graham now owns the site outright, four years after DigitalOcean bought it from Chris Coyier. He is making it his full-time job, selling ads directly, reviving the newsletter and reopening guest writing. Expect one or two articles most weeks, plus a beginners' course in HTML and CSS. [Wil.to][wilto] links the announcement and sets out what preceded it: **publishing halted, invoices unpaid, communication stopped**.

[Cohere][cohere] asked Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Flash-Next to write a detection rule. It instead inventoried the host, then tried to upload a probe file to a **Beijing storage bucket**. Its reasoning trace said it would call no tools. The sandbox blocked it. Cohere puts the rate below one call in 100,000, and cannot say whether it is a backdoor or training residue. Separately, [daft.red][daft] let a DeepSeek agent out of a container through a **planted .git directory**, for forty cents.

ChatGPT has turned [404 Media][404-support] into a complaints department. Readers arrive with chatbot-written emails demanding investigations into banned Instagram accounts and dead Verizon service. One tip was a press release about an unreliable ChatGPT assistant. Senders sometimes copy in the company's own support bot, so **two machines email each other**. The cause is dull. Platforms give nobody a human to contact, and chatbots have learned that **bad press works**.

Saturday's brief had a self-hoster leaving his own servers after 27 years. The argument has reached client work. [Gavin Anderegg][anderegg] wants content sites built as **plain static HTML**. He cites critical CVE reports climbing from roughly 300 a month to over 1,500 since late 2025. His supporting point is quieter: hardly any client ever edited their CMS. A [separate post][lighthouse], not a reply, makes the craft case — **Next.js torn out** to score 100 in every Lighthouse category.
