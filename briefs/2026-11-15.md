# Feedbox brief — Sunday 15 November 2026

Friday's ground stop has turned into a row about its cause. Sean Duffy first blamed overloaded switches and routers. Then he blamed the firm swapping copper for fibre, [Common Dreams][duffy] reports. [L3Harris][l3h] confirmed the work and said it is **helping the FAA find out why**. It did not accept blame. A Washington Examiner writer asked 60 controllers. **None believed the account**, saying such swaps are done at night, midweek. A GAO report in September warned of just this.

The human-made line ran on from Thursday, now as a row over disclosure. [ttvl.co][ttvl] says the asterisk on "I made this" is not needed if you own the work. **Nobody asterisks a compiler, a chisel or a drill press.** [Questions Considered][qc] quotes ACM's James Larus, who draws the line elsewhere. Use AI to voice your own thinking, not to skip it. [One journal entry][baty] counts the cost. Its author suspects people have **stopped writing**, over some LLM-built scripts.

Friday's brief covered Bubbles dropping comments for links between blogs. That swap now has a visible use. [Newsonaut][newsonaut] replies to the very post that announced it. It was sorry to lose comments, but takes the trade: **replies invite thought and end drive-by hits**. Ben Behnke, who runs Bubbles, has also [added a Spanish edition][es]. It lists about **220 blogs from Spain and Latin America**, seeded from a directory readers sent him.

Three posts, Saturday and Sunday, make one case from three crafts: the doing is the point. [One argues for slow work by hand][nk]. It bars AI from writing and keeps friction, because **doing a thing again is what makes it good**. [Another rebuilds keyboards][kasson], then gives most away. The parallel it draws is **a sand mandala, swept up once done**. The [Brick Experiment Channel][bec] finds its Lego ideas in daydreamed "what if" questions.

## Talking points

1. **The data centre bill lands on home energy bills.** Global Action Plan told the London Assembly that growth on plan could push 77,000 UK homes into fuel poverty. About 10,000 of those are in London, and the group puts £88 on the mean bill. Water UK put the extra draw at up to 77 million litres a day. [The Standard][standard]

2. **Ten years of blogging with low vision beats any overlay sales pitch.** The anniversary post sets out the theme settings and plain CSS it leans on, in place of overlay tools, which it says break sites. [Veroniiiica][vero]

3. **The whole component may be the wrong thing to ship.** One case says build from parts — pop-up placing, focus, choice — not from whole dropdowns, so that agents can read and drive them too. [Amit Merchant][amit]

4. **The DRAM supply case will go ahead in full.** A judge in California let a class claim against Samsung, SK Hynix and Micron stand. The court found HBM lock-ups and flat capacity at record prices make a deal plausible. Answers are due 7 December. [Litnews][dram]
