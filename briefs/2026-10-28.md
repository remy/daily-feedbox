# Feedbox brief — Wednesday 28 October 2026

Apple was told on 17 October that eMudhra's keys had leaked. By Wednesday, **git, curl and python3 as shipped with macOS still trusted the bad certs**. A [tracker site][emudhra] gives a one-line check, plus a live demo that fakes github.com. macOS 27.2 did not fix it. Worse, it **undid the fix people had applied by hand**. [Apple's own note][apple] says a patch waits on a later update, and tells anyone who skips Terminal to do nothing.

Someone crawled 10,000 sites to gather browser fingerprint canvases. [One script][nullpt] held names such as `computeRicinLethalDosageForWaterSupply`. Hidden in blank Unicode sat a fake system prompt. It asked for bioweapon and tax-dodge guides. **The harm is the point.** It is there to make a model refuse to read the file. Two models said no. A third went ahead. Code built to trip up models now ships on live sites.

Wednesday was also a day of people tending their own sites. Christian Heilmann is [moving 21 years of blogging][heilmann] off WordPress, pushed out by **GoDaddy charging for a cert that Let's Encrypt made free**. attronarch [got comments working again][attronarch] after the old system died without notice and took every comment with it. And candyether.space [answered][candyether] Tuesday's [case for polished writing][khleedril]: **not everyone can write well, and that should not decide who gets seen.**

Saying no to generated code keeps hardening into rules. Sunday's brief ran the r/opensource ban. Tuesday's ran Strudel's new policy. [AlpineBSD][alpine] builds the stance into a whole system. The stated aim is **to use as little AI-made software as it can**: a NetBSD kernel, an Alpine-style userspace on apk and busybox, and **no LLM output in the base system or the main package set**. There is no installer yet.

## Talking points

1. **A website spec you can audit against.** [One new site][spec] breaks a good website into 178 checks across ten areas. Each one is a yes or no. 36 are required and 6 are things to stop doing. It also serves itself over a read-only MCP server. That tells you who it is for.
2. **Your chatbot wrote your profile without asking.** Ben Werdmuller [reads a study][werd] where 96 per cent of ChatGPT memories were made by the bot alone. 52 per cent held guesses about the user's mind. His point lands. These are products, not friends, and the same firms sell to the police.
3. **Gothic revival runs on CNC.** Monks [carve pinnacles by machine][cnc], turning months of chisel work into days. The case is sound. Medieval builders used the newest tools they could get, so the romance was never in the chisel.
4. **Incident response is mostly about talking well.** Ben Tasker [puts the fix second][bentasker]: declare early, keep the timeline, guard the team's focus. Read it on a quiet day, because nobody reads it on a loud one.
