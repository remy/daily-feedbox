# Feedbox brief — Monday 16 November 2026

Monday brought causes for both failures Friday's and Sunday's briefs ran, and they share a shape. Microsoft blames a **defect shipped in July**, latent in its gateway servers. Load testing had favoured weaker machines with fewer cores, so the lock contention never showed ([The Register][register-ms]). **Over 80% of servers went unresponsive**, and the defect was too old to roll back. The FAA's is smaller and worse: contractors swapped one router in Arkansas, which flooded the backbone ([The Verge][verge-faa]).

The new detail is what the outage cost away from a keyboard. A dosing system at a Phantasialand hotel pushed too much chlorine into the pool. The **safety alarm went out by email**. Nobody read it. Fifteen guests were treated for airway irritation and four went to hospital, reported [WDR][wdr]. The pool water was replaced. [The Verge][verge-chlorine] paired it with the incident report.

The Nvidia thread ran on hard from Thursday's brief. Trump said a Huang return "certainly isn't a bad idea" ([The Verge][verge-trump]). The board is **not considering it**, a source told CNBC, and has hired Wachtell Lipton to investigate him ([The Verge][verge-board]). A petition past 180,000 names has Musk and Tim Sweeney on it, and also "Xi Jingping" ([The Verge][verge-plotting]). Senator Maria Cantwell accused Commerce of leaving **open pathways** to Chinese buyers ([The Verge][verge-cantwell]).

Bear Blog shipped an API, and the reaction took a day. Posts, pages, drafts, navigation and custom CSS are all reachable with a key, explains [widdershins][widdershins]. [Robert Birming][birming] had published a dashboard plugin by Monday. It lists every post's tags and meta description, flags the gaps, and merges tags that differ only by case. [Kev Quirk][kevquirk] built Pure Blog around that gap, and says he would not have bothered.

## Talking points

1. **The replies-versus-comments framing is changing other people's sites.** [Ram Kumar][ramkumar] read the Bubbles argument that Friday's and Sunday's briefs covered, then rebuilt his webmentions around it. Social replies are now links out, not reproduced text. He argues that is the honest privacy position too.
2. **A model found a one-click universal XSS in uBlock Origin.** A malicious page could drive the extension's hidden element picker and add a trusted filter, which injects JavaScript anywhere. GPT-5.6 found it, [the author][ubo] verified it, Claude wrote the proof of concept. It is fixed.
3. **Nearly one Amazon "deal" in three saved nobody anything.** [Groundwork Collaborative][groundwork] priced over 350 products from Amazon's own Black Friday and Cyber Monday pages. Nearly one in ten cost more than it had a month earlier.
4. **The text caret is a focus indicator, and it has contrast requirements.** [Sara Soueidan][caret] makes the point that lands: hide focus rings from pointer users with `:focus-visible`, and the blinking caret is all they get.
