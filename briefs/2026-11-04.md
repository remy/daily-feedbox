# Feedbox brief — Wednesday 4 November 2026

Data centres turned into an election issue. [The Verge][verge-dc] counted 45 local ballot measures, and 33 of those will restrict development. An NBC exit poll put opposition at 76 per cent. [404 Media][404-dc] tracked the Texas result: Greg Abbott lost by more than 700,000 votes, after taking over $9m from data-centre donors. [CNBC][cnbc] saw CoreWeave drop 4.2 per cent before the open. Sunday's brief read the AI bill as a markets story. It is now a ballot one.

Google still cannot make AI earn like search ads. [The Verge][verge-google] reports search revenue growth down to 12 per cent, from roughly 17 the quarter before. Paid clicks slowed from 13 per cent to 8. Alphabet shares have fallen about 14 per cent since the earnings call, against some **$200bn of planned capital spending** this year. [Futurism][futurism] relays a New York Times report on the other end of that squeeze. Google will cut off Search for schools that ban AI.

Safari 27.3 beta can darken sites that never built a dark theme. [WebKit][webkit] says almost **40 per cent of page loads come from dark-mode users**, while only 10 per cent of loads support it. A colour matrix inverts luminosity and keeps hue, aiming at the WCAG 4.5:1 ratio. It inspects SVGs and reads pixel histograms to rescue logos. Pages that declare `color-scheme: light dark` are left untouched. [512 Pixels][512px] and [Caleb Hailey][caleb] both quote the post.

Two posts pulled home infrastructure in opposite directions. [Off Grid Holdout][ogh] runs five Debian machines at home, behind one nginx ingress and a Tailscale mesh. One of them is a BBS on an emulated vintage PC. [Scatter Splatter][scatter] went the other way, leaving a VPS for shared hosting on NearlyFreeSpeech.NET. The stated reason: **"AI is finding vulnerabilities quickly these days"**. A static site made the move an rsync and a DNS change.

## Talking points

1. **C++26 was voted down.** The ISO national bodies disapproved the draft on 23 October. [Andreas Fertig][fertig] believes it is the first time a C++ ballot has failed. Contracts is the sticking point. He sits on the committee and votes for Germany.
2. **A cease and desist killed an open-source driver.** [Phoronix][phoronix] reports that Synaptics used patent claims against Vino, a clean-room DisplayLink driver. The repository has gone from GitHub. The older USB2 hardware had open drivers; the new IP will not.
3. **Ladybird moved style and layout off the main thread.** [Its October notes][ladybird] say CSS animations and `:hover` keep working while JavaScript blocks. The team claims no other browser does this. It also passed Safari Technology Preview on Web Platform Tests.
4. **The hand cursor won.** The CSS Working Group chose `cursor: pointer` for `appearance: base` form controls. [A Beautiful Site][cursors] notes that Nicole Sullivan found 27 of 33 design systems already set it, though a wider sample evened the split.
