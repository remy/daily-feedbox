# Feedbox brief — Monday 5 October 2026

Meta's Muse is back, this time through a leak. [404 Media][muse] reports that Meta engineers found **VM escape bugs** in the weeks before launch. A source says protections were rushed. Meta's own post cites a "sudden spike in reported KVM escapes". This continues Saturday's Muse thread. On the Apple side, [Warner Crocker][crocker] notes that Apple will add **explicit controls for Full Disk Access**. He worries the Mac may drift towards iOS.

arXiv now limits authors to **two submissions a month**, with three active at once. [404 Media][arxiv] says it received 40,363 papers in September 2026, against 20,569 in September 2024. Its moderators are volunteers, and it cites "thin papers of narrow scope" and dense AI-written text. A short [post][slop] shows the same cost at small scale. Colleagues paste long chatbot answers into Slack and expect them to be read.

A CSS trick fills a gap in the platform. `sibling-count()` counts siblings, not children, and the proposed `children-count()` is still under discussion. A [post on blog.master.dev][sibling] **transfers the value to the parent** using scroll-driven animations. Only Chrome and Edge support the method. On Firefox, [Mark Tomlinson][nova] gives the `about:config` switch for the rounded tabs. Thursday's brief ran the complaints about them.

Two web workers take opposite tones on AI at work. [Henry][forestry] writes that **"we don't write code anymore"** and that ethics no longer pay. The refrain is "Gotta pay the bills". [Chris Coyier][coyier] is lighter. He says he hates AI slop writing and will not automate it. He does like a one-click WordPress MCP for **rote tasks** such as checking links.

## Talking points

1. **Altman is making harm an explicit trade.** He tells Politico's podcast that the world should accept "some bad things happening". He says that sets OpenAI apart from Anthropic, and calls OpenAI "pragmatic centrists". [The Verge][altman] sets it beside David Robinson's resignation on Saturday.

2. **Denmark's population register was read through a supplier.** The CPR says outsiders misused a Danish firm's legitimate search access. About 8.8 million names, addresses and CPR numbers were exposed. Protected-address records were not. [CPR notice][cpr] (in Danish).

3. **GrapheneOS may skip the Pixel 11.** The team says its port stalls on missing ARM memory tagging support. It calls the cut a cost-saving move by Google. Tagging has been in the project since the Pixel 8. [GrapheneOS forum][pixel]

4. **A government proxy served a personal blog for free.** New Zealand's zero-rated portal fetched and restyled any site on request. The author beat its banner with `!important`. The portal later swapped hashes for readable names. [Serving my blog via govt.nz][govt]
