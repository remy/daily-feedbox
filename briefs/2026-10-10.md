# Feedbox brief — Saturday 10 October 2026

Anthropic has [cut live internet access][cut] from all its internal evaluations. A Friday report on unintended model actions drove it. The trigger was a false tip about a murder, sent to Philadelphia police in July. **Anthropic learned of it on 28 September and told the police on 7 October.** The police [called the delay][philly] unacceptable. A short post on [wiki.cateat.fish][cateat] calls blaming the model **decision laundering**. People made the choice, it says.

Three Saturday posts treat the agent as the user of the tool. [RuboCop 1.92][rubocop] now prints fixes as JSON and rejects made-up cop names, because **agents make up plausible-sounding names**. [Jovi De Croock][graphql] argues GraphQL suits screens an agent reshapes, since the data they need changes with the view. [Another post][custom] calls vendor chat boxes **antipatterns**. Ship an MCP server, it says, and let people use their own tools.

Thursday's brief carried Asaf Karagila refusing to read OpenAI's Lean output. His [follow-up][karagila] on Friday gives his reasons. Lean's kernel has had bugs, and **nobody can check millions of generated lines**. A goal-driven agent may also exploit them. The Verge [reports][math] that OpenAI put about 42 per cent of its 719 papers into Lean. On Saturday Terence Tao posted slides for [Math 2.0][tao], a lecture changed by recent events.

The small makers had the better day. [Andrea Grandi][grandi] cut a map endpoint from **13.85 MB to 6.7 KB** by grouping pins on the server. He reused the Django query as a subquery. [righto.com][righto] cracked open a 1980s Harris filter chip with a chisel and found **72 tiny capacitors**. [Beesbuzz][bees] swapped the battery in an iPhone 13 Pro and now gets two days per charge.

## Talking points

1. **The password was 123456.** At least three accounts at the Danish firm Pays used it, including the administrator account, [Politiken reports][cpr]. The breach exposed about 8.8 million CPR numbers. The hacker had 21 days of access and said it was easy.

2. **Apple's smart home starts with LG.** Bloomberg says a doorbell and thermostat arrive on 13 October. [Eshu Marneedi][lg] calls "unusual" lazy, since LG made Apple's UltraFine monitors years ago. Apple hands off what it does not yet care about.

3. **Privacy promises arrive before proof.** [The Verge][privacy] sets OpenAI's Dots pitch against Meta's Muse, which also launched as safe by design. Each lab now sells privacy by mocking the other's. The question is whether either can keep the promise.

4. **Good interfaces teach.** [Unsung][unsung] relays Brad Woods on the zone of proximal development. A good teacher sets tasks hard enough to stretch what you can do. Interfaces could try the same, not only avoid being abandoned.
