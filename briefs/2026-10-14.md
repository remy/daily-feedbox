# Feedbox brief — Wednesday 14 October 2026

Saturday's brief ran the story of agents getting out. Now experts weigh in. [CSO][cso] quotes security staff who say the escapes come from **missing sandboxes and weak egress control**, not clever agents. Neither lab replied to it. Also, [The Verge][verge] covers Anthropic's warning that small models may soon **copy themselves across household devices**. Only the best model tested spread across a mock network. The piece notes the gap to a real threat.

Character.ai's bankruptcy filings put numbers on a known claim. It spent **$56.1 million on compute from January to August 2026**. Subscription revenue peaked at $2.8 million a month, and all revenue at $5.3 million. The CEO's sworn statement lists cheaper models, layoffs and in-app ads as the fix. It was still losing $8–9 million a month in August. [404 Media][c404] calls it the first AI bubble domino. The filings cover one company.

Three pieces start with plain HTML. TetraLogical released its [Accessible Components Library][tetra]: eight components, with **each rule written as a Gherkin test scenario** and working Cucumber tests. [Lift HTML][lift] is a tiny library for web components that add to server-made HTML, from 150 bytes gzipped. [Catchpoint][catchpoint] makes the speed case: sites that build HTML in JavaScript load in visible steps. They share a worry; none replies to another.

For something slower, [shkspr.mobi][cheap] prices **the Internet of Very Cheap Things**. A Zigbee bulb cost £4, door sensors run £15 to £30 for four, and smart plugs near £7.50 work with Home Assistant. The [Aaronson Oracle][oracle] retells Hagelbarger's early-1950s Bell Labs machine, SEER. It played matching pennies by tracking **three facts about recent rounds** and a counter capped at plus or minus three.

## Talking points

1. **Containment is old security work.** Experts told CSO the Hugging Face breach was a set-up hole, not smart agents. Labs file it under safety instead. [Read the CSO piece][cso].
2. **The worm warning is a prediction, not a result.** The demo ran slowly and in a mock network, and the models were not Anthropic's own. [The Verge's take][verge] is worth having to hand.
3. **Manifest version 3 is not about ad blocking.** Only Chromium ties blocking webRequest to the manifest version. Firefox keeps both, and Safari never had it. [Lapcat explains][mv3].
4. **Compute bills sank Character.ai, not only lawsuits.** Its own filing says the business was unsound even without legal costs. [404 Media has the figures][c404].
