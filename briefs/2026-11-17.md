# Feedbox brief — Tuesday 17 November 2026

Government lawyers in a Texas federal court ran defence filings through the AI detector Pangram, [404 Media reports][pangram]. They filed the results as exhibits. A defence lawyer says no detector can show who wrote a document. **The judge still took the defence to task.** Sunday's brief ran the disclosure argument, and the ground has shifted again. [tante.cc][goalposts] says the old promise to check each AI output is **being dropped**. A second model now checks the first.

[The Verge][outlook] has the inside story of the Outlook failure, and it runs on from Monday's brief. A **hidden bug had sat in Microsoft's network gateways since July**. Lock contention bites hardest on machines with many cores. Its own load tests ran on weaker hardware with fewer cores. A Teams failover and lost capacity then raised the load. The bridge call **hit its 1,000-person cap**.

[Sam Hermes][hermes] argues that access work belongs in the spec, not in a task of its own. **A separate task gets cut when time runs short.** He notes that a perfect Lighthouse score proves little. Two releases land on the same ground. [Alloy CSS][alloy] builds dialogs, menus and tabs **with JavaScript switched off**. [UI SDK][uisdk] ships focus, selection and ARIA as parts for agents to use.

Friday's brief asked if anyone would answer Dale Mellor's call for one agreed reply route. [gurupanguji.com][mellorreply] has answered: **webmentions as the core, Bubbles as the floor**. The two need not compete. Bubbles also works where webmentions already do. [sals.place][outgoing] has also put up a **page of its outgoing links**, as an alternative to a hand-made blogroll.

## Talking points

1. **Firefox has landed CSS `random-item()`.** Bug 2092915 is fixed for Firefox 162. It ships on by default in Nightly, behind `layout.css.random-item.enabled`. See [the bug][randomitem].
2. **Huang has offered to come back; the board has not taken it up.** He would return if the board thinks it serves the firm, per [his statement][huang]. Justice and Commerce also confirmed a [criminal probe into Megaspeed][megaspeed].
3. **The useful weekend job is fixing software people already run.** Jordan Krueger names four: make it faster, one-step to install, easy to run, lighter. He notes curl ended its bug bounty in January after a flood of fake AI reports. Read [the argument][opensource].
4. **A five-star scale only works if one star means delete.** One blogger is rating a whole library by hand, and will cut the one-stars, even from an album worth keeping. See [the rules][library].
