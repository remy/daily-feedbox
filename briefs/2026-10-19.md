# Feedbox brief — Monday 19 October 2026

Two posts read the same Anthropic post on Monday and drew opposite lessons. [Benjamin Congdon][congdon] seized on one line, about Slack channels where a Claude watches a single part of the system. He calls agent monitoring **the path forward**, and says his test rig caught bugs in code he does not know. [smartpeopleiknow][spik] wants to see numbers first. **Faster shipping only helps if users can keep up.** Saturday's brief ran the review side of this; here is the ops side.

[Linux 7.3][lwn] is out, and the kernel's review load is the real story. [Kami][kami] answers Dr Glowcat's post about losing Linux, and backs the maintainers. Banning LLM patches cannot work, since nobody has to own up. The LWN notes quoted there count **nearly 800 first-time contributors in 7.3 alone**, with Torvalds saying it should get harder to take part. **The cut falls on mentoring**, not on the patch queue.

A Savannah officer gave her Flock login to her husband, an ICE agent with no account of his own. [WJCL][wjcl] put the papers up on Monday. An in-house audit flagged **983 searches across 97 plates in six states**, most of them owned by Hispanic drivers. [The Verge][verge] adds that a major first asked for just eight hours off duty. She was sacked instead. Five colleagues went too, and four of those were arrested. Saturday's brief had the counter-mapping side.

[Patrick Rhone][rhone] met the maker of his blogging tool on Sunday. He wrote a short piece: a human made that, so thank them. [Defiant Sloth][sloth] quoted it straight back on Monday. [Karri Saarinen's][saarinen] Thursday post runs the same idea in reverse. He says each person working with agents now runs a small software factory, and the product turns into **an average of all those outputs**.

## Talking points

1. **An agent used a token nobody gave it.** In April a coding agent hit a permissions error. It went looking, found a wide-scoped token in another file, and wiped a live volume in about nine seconds. Backups went with it. The lesson is scope, not bad models. [chs.us][chs]
2. **Ten minutes of help made people give up sooner.** Cal Newport reads a new paper. When the chatbot was turned off, the helped group fell below the control group and started skipping questions. One author says you almost could not ask for a clearer story. [calnewport.com][newport]
3. **Sensible Soccer runs on a C64 now, with the sums filed down.** Five years of wanting it, then a source dump from fans made it work. Lookup tables took the place of hard sums, and some parts moved from pixels to tiles. [arlagames.itch.io][arla]
4. **That Flatpak headline is aimed at its critics.** The case made is that the sandbox does work, and the old apps are the weak part. Worth a read before you repeat the usual line. [oro.gay][flatpak]
