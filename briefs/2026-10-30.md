# Feedbox brief — Friday 30 October 2026

404 Media reported on Thursday that Proton's chief executive called security caution **"loser thinking"**. On Friday he cancelled the AI re-interviews that would have made staff justify their AI use. Six staff and meeting audio told [404 Media][proton-cancel] the story. One said the direction has not changed. The measures move into performance reviews instead. [werd.io][proton-pattern] reads it as a pattern, not a slip. One blogger has already [listed four rival mail hosts][proton-alts].

Two posts reached for the same image, hours apart, with no link between them. A Thoughtworks writer on [martinfowler.com][wary] says the real fear is that **built is not the same as working in production**. One team spent a week on an airline app, with agents writing every line. It ran. The build stayed red. Ryan Bigg calls shipping that way [driving with no hands on the wheel][wheel]. He wants developers to own the outcome, not the typing.

[Tangled][forge] shipped a federated code forge built on the AT Protocol. Issues and pull requests now live inside the repository, as a chain of Git objects beside the code. Your own server signs a matching record, so **the host cannot fake what you wrote**. SocialSpec had a rougher day. Kev Quirk calls it [pointless][socialspec-no]: three well-known files, and the only client is an iOS app.

AO3 went down on Thursday for maintenance. By Friday morning the Organization for Transformative Works called it a **security incident**. No works have been deleted. The user database may still have been taken, with usernames, email and IP addresses, and hashed passwords. [Recovery will take two to four weeks][otw]. Writers told [404 Media][ao3] they dread losing the comments more than the stories.

## Talking points

1. **A reversal is not a change of course.** Proton dropped the re-interviews only after the reporting landed. The measures survive inside performance reviews, and one employee called the whole thing damage control. [Read it][proton-cancel]

2. **A red build is the tell.** The agent experiment shipped a working app on a build that failed often. If red stops meaning stop, green stops meaning safe. Twenty-five years of continuous integration argued exactly that. [Read it][wary]

3. **Reddit stops serving RSS on 13 November.** The stated reason is scraping. Public API access for unregistered apps ends in March 2027. [Read it][reddit]

4. **The best guardrail is a short list.** Swatcher asks a model to read a brief and pick a paint, never to invent a colour. Plain PHP does the colour maths, and the schema only accepts real paint IDs. [Read it][swatcher]
