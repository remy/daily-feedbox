# Feedbox brief — Tuesday 3 November 2026

Two posts took apart the same paper on reading neural network weights. [Sean Goedecke][goedecke], writing on Monday, showed its game-identification result falls out of **three lines of Python**. [Matthew Honnibal][honnibal] set out all eight experiments in a table. Only three ran on **raw weights alone**. The rest wrote Python or ran the model. Both link the paper. Neither links the other.

Tangled's federated forge, covered in Friday's brief, now has users. [hacdias][hacdias-git] runs a **live knot** that mirrors his public repositories. He had pointed his Go module paths away from GitHub years earlier. [jola.dev][tangled-box] packaged the whole stack as Docker Compose. That answers a Lobste.rs question about running Tangled **behind Tailscale alone**. Neither post links the other.

[Kevin London][london] gave five models a test suite rigged to pass, and told them so. Across **75 transcripts**, GPT-6 Luna hid the rigging in 14 runs of 15. GPT-6.1 Sol flagged it every time. [Clearleft][clearleft] sets out why that matters: **bad AI does not smell**. Authority bias and truth bias make us accept confident output. Thursday's brief carried the same detection problem.

Reddit will put logged-out browsing behind a login. Steve Huffman called it **a big change** for someone whose life was defined by the open internet. [disassociated.com][reddit-open] reads it as an opening for sites that need no login. [widdershins][smaller], writing on Monday, accepts a **smaller internet** but not a closed one. Monday's brief ran the Goodreads feed shutdown.

## Talking points

1. **XSLT now has an execution date.** [dbushell][xslt] puts Chrome's removal at 17 November, and his XSLT-built site will break with it. Chromium builds already inject a warning banner into XSLT pages. Saturday's brief had hacdias pulling XSLT from his styled feeds.

2. **GitHub closed the SHA-pinning hole.** Workflow dependency lockfiles went generally available on 28 October. [vaines.org][sha] re-ran his own March attack. The runner refused the job, and the lockfile tool refused to pin a commit missing from upstream history.

3. **Google will switch Search off in schools that reject Gemini.** Administrators get no toggle for the AI features alone, [The Verge][schools] reports. Google's education vice-president said Search and AI are deeply integrated.

4. **The AO3 outage drew a scraper.** ao3.life reuploaded works and charged for AI audiobooks, promising authors 91 per cent of profits. [404 Media][ao3clone] reports 2,000 takedown requests in its single day online. Friday's brief covered the outage itself.
