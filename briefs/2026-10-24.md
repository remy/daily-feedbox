# Feedbox brief — Saturday 24 October 2026

The compression bug behind [Node2Shell][node2shell] is much wider than Node. Aikido reports that CVE-2026-111497 sits in Google's Brotli decoder itself. That drags in Python, C++, curl, Bun, Envoy and Istio. **OpenAI found the flaw in February.** The root cause was fixed in source then, but nobody filed it as a security issue, so no release carried it. Advisory databases still do not map the CVE to the PyPI packages, so **Dependabot stays quiet**.

Matt Webb [shut his web servers down][bunker] on Thursday, after 27 years of running his own. He assumed the boxes were already compromised, and took nothing off them. The blog now sits on GitHub Pages. **His reason is arithmetic, not panic.** Patching is still possible, but the time no longer pays. [Ton Zylstra][zylstra], who left his own hardware in 2007, says cutting attack surface was on his mind as well.

Aaron Gustafson's [ASSETS paper][pace] puts hard numbers on generated accessibility. Its PACE testbed crossed five models, 58 form components and three levels of accessibility guidance. That gave 26,100 outputs. The result is awkward: **more guidance does not reliably help**. DeepSeek improved steadily as instructions got firmer. Gemini 3.1 moved the other way, its schema score dropping from 0.84 unguided to 0.65 under expert guidance. **There is no magic prompt.**

Julik Tarkhanov names what agents quietly took away: [the duty to persuade anyone][julik]. The maintainer who closed your pull request, the guild that needed six weeks — you can route around all of them in an afternoon. His worry is not that the rewrites come out bad. They are often fine. It is that **the mountain that forced people to work together has gone**, and the open source bargain was built on it.

## Talking points

1. **Europe has learned who holds the switch.** Steve Blank, a month into a London stay, says the Starshield throttling turned a paranoid fantasy into a procurement question. European founders now get [sovereignty as a sellable feature][blank].
2. **Ten of 44 references could not be traced.** A peer-reviewed librarianship paper cites a TorrentFreak piece that [was never published][zlib]. Its reference entry links to the homepage. The acknowledgements mention AI.
3. **A functional language got within 3% of hand-tuned C.** Richard Feldman's Roc port of DEFLATE ran a median 2.6% slower than libdeflate on an M1, and [8.9% slower on a Ryzen][roc]. Bounds checks cost less than the folklore says.
4. **One post made Kev Quirk reverse himself.** Pure Comments will [drop Fediverse integration entirely][purecomments]. The Webmention spec calls them notifications, he notes, never comments.
