# Feedbox brief — Monday 2 November 2026

Saturday's brief ran October's disclosure pile-up. The sequel is the patch queue. [PostgreSQL 19 is late][postgres] because LLMs keep finding bugs. Back-patched commits rose by **more than 60% in a year**. Commits to the prior stable branch rose 74%. Drafting fixes got easier; reviewing them did not. [chalmovsky.com][ubuntu] shows the other half. A kernel TCP flaw rated 9.8 has sat there since 2016. Debian patched on 16 October. **Ubuntu was still listed vulnerable** on 2 November.

The exposure already shipped is the harder problem. Supermarket freezers in Sweden, Belgium and Luxembourg [warmed up on Monday][freezers]. Dozens of stores were hit. This is the **second such wave**. French police said up to 700 supermarkets lost cooling in October. Attackers disabled the alarms first. Then they pushed thermostats from -27C to over 50C. The advice in [Daniel Miessler's security floor][floor] is blunt. List everything you have shipped, then **get it off the open internet**.

A computer scientist writing at [remy.wang][hilbert] reaches back to Hilbert. Gödel and Turing killed the dream of a proof machine. AI agents with Lean have now built one. His worry is code, not maths: the habit of **not reading it** is spreading. [fev.al][clankers] makes the same case from the on-call rota. Mark Seemann supplies the reason. An abstraction must translate **precisely and deterministically**. [LLM prompting does neither][abstraction]. The layer below still needs reading.

Goodreads has switched off RSS feeds. [somebits.com][goodreads] says the bargain is **broken** and now self-hosts its book reviews. StoryGraph has roughly 5 million users; Goodreads has 150 million. A web archivist offers a different answer: [back up the sites you love][backup] yourself, in WARC format. [Elseweb][elseweb] takes a third route. It is a **curated feed of personal blogs**, each one reviewed before listing.

## Talking points

1. **Masonry in CSS is called grid lanes now.** Rachel Andrew [reports from TPAC in Dublin][css]. The working group settled its reading-flow values: `grid-lanes-flow` and `grid-lanes-track`. Firefox is also [taking developer votes][interop] on the Interop 2027 list.

2. **Interview the candidate, not the code.** [seldo.com proposes][juniors] an hour with one model playing the customer and a second writing the code. Employment of developers aged 22 to 25 is down nearly 20% from late 2022.

3. **Add one line to your agent instructions.** [paulca.com asked a room][a11y] how many had an accessibility standard in AGENTS.md. Almost nobody did. Accessible software is also software an agent can drive.

4. **The detectors cannot do this job.** [sciencebase.com tested][detector] a tool advertised as 100% accurate. Pre-LLM writing came back as probably AI. Lightly edited machine text came back as fully human.
