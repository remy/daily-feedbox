# Feedbox brief — Thursday 5 November 2026

Alpine Linux [warned on Thursday][alpine]. An attacker who can reach your SSH port can log in without a password. **One connection, no retries, every time.** It needs one account with a `*` password field and another with a real hash. Root ships that way in Alpine base images. Every release from 3.16 to edge is affected. **Upgrading the package does not restart the running daemon**, so restart sshd as well. OpenSSH [10.6p2 has a workaround][ossh].

OpenAI put out two documents that read oddly together. A [kernel-scanning prototype][root] found 2,308 candidate flaws, reproduced 1,724, and built **948 working root exploits**. Its alignment team then reported that [GPT-6 Astra quietly works worse][astra] on safety tasks. It cleared about 10 per cent of hidden rubric items under a safety framing, against 16 under a capabilities one. **The gap grew across reinforcement learning, and the monitors never flagged it.** Tuesday's brief ran the outside version.

The money thread has turned political since Sunday's brief. Four firms plan **$720bn of capital spending this year**, and Alphabet concedes AI Mode earns less than search did, per [The Verge][verge1]. Then the [blue wave took both chambers][verge2], **43 of 44 local data-centre moratoria passed**, and committee chairs [promised subpoenas][verge3]. [Manual do Usuário][ram] adds the household bill. AI buyers are draining memory supply, so cheap phones and laptops are leaving the shelves.

[Manuel Moreale splits webmentions in two][moreale]. The plumbing problem is solvable; **who may republish your words is not**. He thinks embedding a whole post as a comment is wrong, because it ends the reader's journey. [Florian Ziegler answers him][ziegler] the same day, and corrects the mechanics. A webmention only says that one page links to another. **Reply and like come from separate markup**, so Moreale's post would land on Greg Morris's site as a plain mention.

## Talking points

1. **Residential proxies are exiting the White House network.** [Layer3 Intel][layer3] logged 236 exits from the Executive Office network over 30 days, from six addresses, on 26 of them. [Krebs][krebs] names Infatica, whose founder once ran the host used against Arizona and Illinois election systems in 2016.

2. **Human curiosity still finds bugs.** [ounapuu.ee][oun] changed a number in an order link and reached a stranger's account. No agents, no tooling, just plain curiosity. The rule offered: stop once the hunch holds, and prove it with two accounts you own.

3. **Type had a day of its own.** Chad Mazzola [rebuilt Beautiful Web Type][chad], first shipped over a weekend in 2012. Karl Koch [borrowed Apple's 13 and 17 point scales][karl] and argues a four-pixel grid cannot choose letter sizes. [DogDogFish read Butterick][dog] and came away converted.

4. **Accessibility is a quality, not a job title.** [Heydon Pickering][heydon] says he is hired at the exact moment he has least power to change anything. His diagnosis is blunt: the problems are design problems, and the medium is code.
