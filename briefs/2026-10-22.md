# Feedbox brief — Thursday 22 October 2026

The PHP story ran on from Wednesday's brief, and it became a row over credit. [Shellsharks][chaos] counts Calif, Wiz, OpenAI and Google all claiming the find. It calls the disclosure **botched** and notes no real damage has been reported yet. [Violet Pixel][violet] quotes the PHP Foundation: Chainguard published fixed images **more than six hours early**, which let attackers study the patch before everyone else.

The cheapest defence was to switch off. [Ton Zylstra][ton] took every site offline as a precaution and brought them back once hosting patches landed. The author of [ruk.ca][ruk] stayed **offline for more than 24 hours** and rebuilt on a new Fedora server, because Fedora 42 would never get the fix. The old keep-it-running reflex would have meant an all-nighter, the post says.

Two providers of internet trust stepped back on Thursday. [eMudhra][emudhra], whose weak certificate keys ran in Tuesday's and Wednesday's briefs, will **leave the global TLS certificate business**. It says TLS was about 1% of revenue. [Quad9][quad9] will **pull out of France on 24 November**. A French court ordered it to block domains for the broadcaster beIN, and it will not.

[Piccalil][piccalil] explains `display: grid-lanes`, CSS's native masonry layout. The Working Group settled on it after years of argument over whether masonry belonged inside Grid or in its own mode. It **reuses Grid's track properties**, so lanes need no hand-set item heights. [Artlung][artlung] ranks it third in the 2027 Interop votes.

## Talking points

1. **AI has changed the pace, not the cause.** [404 Media][404] quotes Cloudflare's CEO: thousands of exploit attempts in the ten hours before the PHP news, millions after. The writer's view is that the weakness is old, neglected software, not rogue machines.

2. **Disclosure channels are creaking.** Trend Micro's Zero Day Initiative posted several Linux kernel reports to the public mailing list by mistake, [Phoronix reports][phx]. Two score 9.0, and mainline is not yet patched.

3. **Planning for hours, building in minutes.** Is that waterfall? [build.ms says no][buildms]: wrong designs now cost an afternoon, not months. [Idiallo][idiallo] sees a man running eight agents with no time to himself, because saved time just buys more output.

4. **Webmentions are notifications, not federation.** [Manuel Moreale][manu] reads the W3C text as covering alerts about links. Moreale rejects the view that anything public is fair to copy: someone who deletes a post should expect it gone.
